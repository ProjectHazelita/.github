# Code of Conduct

Project Hazelita is committed to providing a **friendly, inclusive, gender-friendly, and diversity-respecting** environment for everyone participating in our projects and community spaces.

We welcome developers, users, researchers, learners, contributors, and travelers who happen to stop by.

## Our expectations

When participating in Project Hazelita, please:

- treat others with respect and good faith;
- be considerate of different identities, backgrounds, experiences, and perspectives;
- discuss ideas critically without attacking the people behind them;
- avoid harassment, discrimination, intimidation, or deliberately hostile behavior;
- help keep technical and community spaces welcoming and constructive;
- respect the boundaries and privacy of other community members.

Disagreement is normal. Cruelty is not.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to participation in Project Hazelita repositories and other spaces associated with the Project Hazelita community.

Some spaces may have additional rules or moderation policies appropriate to their purpose.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness behavior that may violate the Community Guidelines, please use the reporting channels described in the Community Guidelines.

Please avoid turning sensitive interpersonal or moderation matters into public GitHub issues unless public discussion is explicitly appropriate.

Reports should be handled with care for the privacy, safety, and dignity of the people involved.
