# Code of Conduct

Project Hazelita is committed to providing a **friendly, inclusive environment that welcomes people of all genders and respects diversity** for everyone participating in our projects and community collaboration. We welcome developers, users, learners, researchers, contributors, and travelers who happen to find their way here.

## Our Shared Ground Rules

When participating in Project Hazelita projects and collaborative spaces, please:

- Respect other people's identities, backgrounds, experiences, views, and boundaries;
- Engage in good faith and try to clarify the issue itself when disagreements arise;
- Feel free to criticize code, work, ideas, and behavior directly and specifically, but do not make personal attacks;
- Do not engage in harassment, discrimination, threats, humiliation, sustained attacks, or other clearly hostile behavior;
- Respect others' privacy, and do not disclose or share personal information or communications that should remain private without permission;
- Stop promptly when explicitly asked to do so, or when maintainers ask for a dispute to end;
- Help maintain a collaborative environment suited to learning, creating, experimenting, and exchanging ideas.

Disagreement is normal, but hurting others is not a way to resolve it.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct provides general guidelines for Project Hazelita's GitHub projects and open-source collaboration. It applies to Issues, Pull Requests, Discussions, code reviews, and other public collaborative spaces in projects maintained by Project Hazelita. Some projects or spaces may establish additional rules for their particular purposes; please also follow any more specific collaboration guidelines that apply to the project.

For the Project Hazelita community's QQ group chat, Discord server, Telegram group, and other community spaces, please also follow the full [Project Hazelita Community Guidelines](https://github.com/ProjectHazelita/community-guidelines).

## Full Community Guidelines

This document is a brief summary for collaboration on GitHub, not a complete set of community rules.

The [Project Hazelita Community Guidelines](https://github.com/ProjectHazelita/community-guidelines) set out the full rules on interactions between members, privacy, boundaries, conflict resolution, moderation authority, handling violations, appeals, and related matters. If this document conflicts with the Project Hazelita Community Guidelines, the Community Guidelines take precedence.

## Reporting and Enforcement

If you experience or witness behavior that may violate this Code of Conduct or the Project Hazelita Community Guidelines, please first contact the relevant project maintainers or the Project Hazelita community moderation team privately.

For harassment, discrimination, privacy issues, conflicts between members, or other sensitive matters, please do not post the information directly in public Issues, Discussions, or Pull Requests to seek help. The procedures for community reports, moderation, and appeals are those described in the Project Hazelita Community Guidelines.

Project Hazelita maintainers may take necessary and appropriate action, depending on the circumstances, in response to content or collaborative behavior that violates this Code of Conduct. Community-level member management, recordkeeping, and appeals should follow the Project Hazelita Community Guidelines. When handling these matters, we will do our best to respect the privacy, safety, and dignity of those involved.
